Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

CalDo is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no CalDo server: the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

Data CalDo accesses

  • Google Calendar. With your permission (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope), CalDo reads your calendars and events to display them, and creates, updates and deletes events when you do so in CalDo.
  • Todoist. If you add a Todoist API token, CalDo reads and updates your tasks and projects.

How the data is used and stored

  • Events and tasks are stored in a local database on your computer so CalDo can work offline.
  • Access tokens are stored in your operating system's keyring (or, if none is available, in a file only your user account can read).
  • CalDo talks only to Google's and Todoist's own APIs, directly from your computer. Your data is not sent to the developer or to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not sold.
  • Your data is used only to provide CalDo's calendar and task features to you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CalDo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

To disconnect CalDo from Google, remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete everything CalDo stored locally, delete its data folder (~/.local/share/caldo on Linux, ~/Library/Application Support/caldo on macOS) and its entries in your keyring.
