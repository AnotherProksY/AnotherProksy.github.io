CalDo

A terminal calendar and task manager with two-way sync to Google Calendar and Todoist.

CalDo runs on your own computer (Linux and macOS). It shows your calendar and your tasks side by side in the terminal, works offline, and syncs changes when you are back online.

What CalDo does with your Google account

When you connect Google Calendar, CalDo asks for access to your calendars so it can show your events and save the events you create, edit or delete in CalDo back to Google Calendar. Your calendar data is stored only on your computer. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
